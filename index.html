Hall of Benevolence and Longevity 仁寿殿

HALL OF BENEVOLENCE & LONGEVITY

This is the main hall of the Summer Palace's court area. Here emperors, and later Empress Dowager Cixi, received officials and handled affairs of state while staying at the palace. It was first built under Emperor Qianlong and was called the Hall of Diligent Government (勤政殿). Anglo-French troops burned it in 1860. When it was rebuilt under Cixi, it received its present name, which comes from the Confucian idea that a kind and benevolent ruler is blessed with a long life. Bronze statues and incense burners stand in the courtyard in front of the hall.
